The problem

Great construction deserves a showcase to match.

  • Your projects stay hidden

    People who want to build search before they call. Without a website, finished projects live in a PDF or loose Instagram photos. A website shows your track record at once.

  • Quotes arrive incomplete

    A message with no type of work, no floor area, no city. On a website, the client already says what they need and your team starts the proposal faster.

  • There is no proof of trust

    Hiring a builder is a big decision. A website with your projects, your team and how you work builds confidence before the first meeting.

About us

FARLO is a two-partner company that builds websites for construction and engineering companies. One handles the conversation and the briefing, the other the production. You talk to people who know that construction sells on trust, and get a site that shows your work and takes the client straight to your WhatsApp.

FAQ

What every construction company asks

What do I need to send?

Photos of your projects (finished and ongoing if possible), a short text about the company, your Instagram handle, your WhatsApp number and your logo, if you have one.

How long does it take?

The timeline is agreed in our conversation and starts counting when you send the complete material.

How does payment work?

50% at the start and 50% when the site goes live.

Is the domain in my name?

Yes. The site address is registered in your name.

What if I want to change something?

Each package has revision rounds: 1 on Essential and 2 on Builder. Corporate is agreed in our conversation.

Do you run ads?

No. FARLO builds the site. It comes with space prepared for measurement, and the setup is done by whoever handles your traffic.
