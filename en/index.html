FAQ

What every business asks

What do I need to send?

Photos of your work or product, a short text about the company, Instagram handle, WhatsApp and logo.

How long does it take?

Agreed in our conversation. The timeline starts when you send the complete material.

How does payment work?

50% at the start and 50% when the site goes live.

Is the domain in my name?

Yes. The address is registered in your name.

What if I want to change something?

Each package has revision rounds: 1 on Essential and 2 on Complete. Corporate is agreed.

Do you run ads?

No. FARLO builds the site, ready for measurement. Setup is done by whoever handles your traffic.

Does 3D make the site heavy?

No. The model only loads when the client asks for it and is optimized to open fast.

Do I need a 3D model ready?

No. We agree on what to model in our conversation. Cost and timeline vary.
