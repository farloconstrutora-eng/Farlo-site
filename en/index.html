FAQ

What every business asks

What do I need to send?

For a website: photos of your work or product, a short text about the company, your Instagram handle, WhatsApp and logo. For a system or automation: a conversation about your routine and what takes your time today.

How long does it take?

Website: your first draft in one week, counted after the deposit and the briefing. Going live depends on the corrections you ask for. Systems and automations have no fixed deadline: they depend on the scope.

How does payment work?

Website: 50% at the start and 50% when it goes live. Systems and automations: price and payment terms are agreed before we start.

Is the domain in my name?

Yes. The address is registered in your company's name.

What if I want to change something?

You can ask for changes as many times as you like. Each revision round is charged separately, at a price agreed in our conversation.

Do you run ads?

No. FARLO delivers the site ready for measurement. Setup is done by whoever handles your traffic.

Does 3D make the site heavy?

No. The model only loads when the visitor asks for it and is optimized to open fast, including on phones.

Do I need a 3D model ready?

No. FARLO models it for you. We agree on what to model in our conversation, and cost and timeline depend on the model.

Does the system work on my phone?

Yes. Websites and systems work on phone and desktop.

Are the CRM and the AI assistant ready?

Not yet. Both are under construction, with no delivery date. Custom systems, such as stock and cash register, orders and scheduling, and everyday automations can already be arranged with us.

Which of the three does your business need first?

Website, system or automation: tell us what your business does and we'll point you to where to start. Build your quote in three questions and we reply on WhatsApp.
